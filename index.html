How to Manage Online Gaming Time Wisely

Step 1 · Read for meaning

Read the text carefully

First, enter your identity in the Teacher tab or here. Your score can be sent to the teacher’s Google Sheet after the Apps Script setup is completed.

Online games are popular among senior high school students because they are entertaining and interactive. However, excessive gaming can affect students’ academic performance, sleep patterns, and mental health. Therefore, managing online gaming time wisely is important.

To begin with, set a clear daily gaming limit. Decide how many hours you are allowed to play and use alarms or app timers to remind you when the time is over.

Next, always complete school assignments and responsibilities before playing games. This helps students stay disciplined and avoid procrastination.

Then, balance gaming with other activities such as exercising, socializing, and resting. Avoid playing games late at night because it can disturb sleep quality.

Finally, reflect on your gaming habits regularly. If gaming starts to interfere with your daily life, reduce the playing time or take a short break from online games.

Reading tip: A procedure text explains how to do something through ordered steps.
